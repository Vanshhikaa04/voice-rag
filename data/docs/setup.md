# Nimbus Hub Setup Guide

## Unboxing and power
The box contains the Nimbus Hub, a USB-C power cable, a 12 watt power adapter and a quick start card. Plug the adapter into a wall outlet and connect the cable to the USB-C port on the back of the hub. The light ring pulses white for about 40 seconds while the hub starts up.

## Connecting to Wi-Fi
Download the Nimbus app from the App Store or Google Play and create an account. Tap Add Device and the app will find the hub over Bluetooth. Choose your home Wi-Fi network and enter the password. The hub supports 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks, but it cannot join enterprise networks that require a login page. When the light ring turns solid green the hub is online.

## Pairing devices
To pair a sensor, bulb or plug, open the app, tap Add Device and put the accessory into pairing mode. For most Nimbus accessories this means holding the accessory button for five seconds until its light blinks blue. The hub supports up to 64 paired devices. Accessories that use Zigbee or Thread are supported, while Z-Wave devices are not.

## Placing the hub
Put the hub in a central spot, off the floor and away from metal cabinets and microwave ovens, which weaken the wireless signal. For best range keep the hub within 10 metres of your farthest accessory, or add a Nimbus Range Extender.
