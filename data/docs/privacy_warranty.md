# Nimbus Hub Privacy and Warranty

## Warranty coverage
Every Nimbus Hub comes with a two year limited warranty starting from the purchase date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware faults. It does not cover water damage, physical damage from drops, or damage caused by unofficial power adapters. To claim, contact support with your order number and a short description of the problem.

## Returns
You can return the hub within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if it is in its original packaging. After 30 days only warranty repairs or replacements are available. Refunds go back to the original payment method within seven business days after the return arrives.

## Data and privacy
Voice commands are processed on the hub and are not sent to the cloud unless you turn on Cloud Voice in the app. Camera and sensor events are encrypted in transit with TLS and stored in the cloud for 30 days by default. You can change the retention period or delete all stored data from Settings, then Privacy. Nimbus never sells personal data to third parties.

## Cloud backups
Automations and device settings are backed up to your account every 24 hours. If you replace your hub you can restore the latest backup from the app during setup.
