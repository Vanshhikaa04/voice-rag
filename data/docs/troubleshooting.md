# Nimbus Hub Troubleshooting

## Light ring colors
A solid green ring means the hub is online. A pulsing white ring means the hub is starting up. A slowly blinking amber ring means the hub has no internet connection. A fast blinking red ring means a hardware fault, and you should contact support. A solid blue ring means the hub is in Bluetooth setup mode.

## Hub is offline
If the ring is blinking amber, first check that your router is working and other devices can reach the internet. Unplug the hub for 10 seconds and plug it back in. If it still cannot connect, move the hub closer to the router and make sure the Wi-Fi password has not changed. Rerun setup in the app if the network name changed.

## Factory reset
To factory reset the hub, press and hold the reset button on the underside for 15 seconds until the light ring flashes red and then turns solid blue. A factory reset removes all paired devices, automations and Wi-Fi settings. Your account and cloud backups are not deleted, so you can restore them after setting the hub up again.

## Devices keep disconnecting
Accessories that drop off the network are usually too far from the hub or running low on battery. Replace batteries in sensors, move the hub closer, or add a Range Extender. Interference from other 2.4 GHz devices such as baby monitors and microwave ovens can also cause drops.

## Firmware updates
The hub installs firmware updates automatically at night between 2 AM and 4 AM when it is idle. To update manually open the app, go to Settings, then Hub, then Check for Updates. Never unplug the hub while the ring is pulsing purple, because that means a firmware update is in progress.
